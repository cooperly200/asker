At present I'm building a classifier to allow the S T A R widget to light up based on where you are in your story, expanding Pipecat-AI's support for Azure AI Foundry, and also experimenting with deployment patterns for small models on Azure.

Data Privacy

Your data, if I can manage to free up the time, will be used solely by me and my wife (a proper datascientist) to train a text (and potentially acoustic) classfier that makes the widget below light up correctly as you tell your STAR story. That means by using this demo, you agree to share your data with us and it will remain exclusively with us.

About that STAR widget

My primary goal with this app is to create a voice experience wherein the user interacts with a voice AI, and UI widget is updated throughout the course of the conversation based on the progress of the conversation. So to play around with it, just say the word "situation", "task", "action", or "result", and system should highlight the appropriate phase.

How to Play

Press connect to begin the interview. The chat bot will lead you through a conversation that has the potential to help you practice telling great STAR stories.
